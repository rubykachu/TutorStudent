# Video and audio conventions

- `script.json` quotes the lesson word for word; a sentence marked `rule` equals a `note` or `caption` of the lesson, and an element of `index.html` that shows rule text carries `data-rule-text`. `pnpm video:check` enforces both and compares the script with the real subtitles.
- An overview narration opens like a video: the first sentence of `overview.hook` greets the child as "bạn" (`pnpm narration:build` stops otherwise; `pnpm video:check` also checks the lead-in). Lessons narrated before the rule carry `"narrationOpeningExempt": true` in `video/projects/<lesson>/media.json` and are never rewritten for it; the rule applies when a lesson's narration is first written.
- Abbreviations are spoken in full: every sentence sent to a TTS engine (video sentences and overview narration, every engine) goes through `spokenText` in `video/lib/text.ts`, which replaces the abbreviations of `SPOKEN_ABBREVIATIONS` (ƯCLN, BCNN, ƯC, BC and the area units cm², m²: "xăng-ti-mét vuông", "mét vuông") and a Roman chapter number after "chương" with the full words, joined by hyphens so the word count stays. Captions and on-screen text keep the abbreviation. Whisper writes the spoken "xăng-ti-mét", "mét" and "mét vuông" as "cm", "m" and "m2"; `wordTokens` in the same file compares them as the syllables. Add a new abbreviation to that map only, never respell it per sentence; the Whisper check and the audio cache key use the spoken form, so only sentences that contain one are synthesized again. `pnpm video:check` and the builds warn (`WARN`) about a capital-letter token the voice would still spell out and about a sentence that introduces an abbreviation ("viết tắt là BC": respell it with `say`, "bê-xê", when the letters are meant).
- If a check forces a change that affects video quality (cutting a sentence, changing pace, dropping an effect), stop and ask the owner.
- Never in git: `video/projects/**/audio/`, `video/projects/**/renders/`, `video/.cache/`, `public/media/`, the TTS environments (`video/.venv`, `video/.venv-omni`, `video/.python`, `video/.hf`). Sentence takes are the source of the narration: do not delete `audio/*.wav` of a finished video, because re-synthesis gives a different voice take.
- A new shared clip prefers a downloaded effect (free licence, short, child-friendly) imported as a `file` entry in `scripts/lib/sound-spec.ts` with its `credit` (title, URL, licence); generate (Gemini voice line or tone) only when none fits.
- Shared app sounds in `public/sounds/` are committed and never regenerated by `pnpm sounds:build` unless a line changed; `manifest.json` hashes are tested.
- A new shared sound effect (UI click, mascot or avatar sound, celebration; not lesson narration or video voices) starts with a ready-made clip from the internet, for example pixabay.com/sound-effects (Pixabay license) or myinstants.com (approved by the owner). Pick short, child-friendly clips and import them through the shared-sound `file` pipeline: copied into `assets/sounds/`, trimmed, loudness-normalized, hashed in `public/sounds/manifest.json`. Record each clip's source URL and license next to its config entry. Generate the sound (Gemini TTS or a synthesized tone) only when no suitable clip exists. Downloading is allowed; never upload. Lesson narration and video voices keep their own pipeline.
- Video engine: a new video reads with OmniVoice (`"engine": "omnivoice"` in `script.json`, cloned from `video/tts/refs/<voice id>.flac` + `.txt`, so the voices keep the names "Hải Đăng" and "Mỹ Duyên"; setup `pnpm video:setup-omni`). The engine is chosen per video in its `script.json`: a video built with VieNeu says `"local"` and keeps VieNeu and its cached takes on a rebuild. The take cache key includes the engine, so switching a video to `omnivoice` re-synthesizes every sentence of it: only when the owner asks, for that video. OmniVoice input always ends in a full stop, question or exclamation mark (`withFinalPunctuation`), because it swallows the last syllable otherwise; a take below the Whisper threshold is synthesized again with a new seed, at most `MAX_REGENERATIONS` times. Trimming, tempo and loudness are the same for every engine.
- OmniVoice weights are CC-BY-NC (non-commercial only; its audio tokenizer under the Boson Higgs Audio 2 Community License). If the app is ever sold, its OmniVoice videos must be read again with another engine.
- One item, one engine: an overview narration or a video is read start to end by one voice. Gemini narration that runs out of quota on every key is read again whole by the lesson's video voice (OmniVoice), never mixed with the Gemini sentences (`video/lib/narration.ts` `readWithFallback`). Gemini keys live only in `~/.config/gemini/`; never print or commit them.
- Writing to the media bucket or any R2 bucket needs the owner's go-ahead each time.
- Pacing for a slow child: a video not listed in `video/pacing-exempt.json` is short, one idea per sentence, asks before it reveals (`pause: "ask"`), and pauses after key reveals (`pause: "think"`); the player never stops by itself, so there are no checkpoints; `video:build` and `video:check` enforce it. Never rebuild an existing video to follow it unless the owner says so.
- First frame of a new video: from frame 0, through the lead-in silence and the greeting, the picture shows the video's title or the example figure next to the owl, never the owl alone. No check enforces it; the author verifies frame `f-000` (skill `lesson-video`, `references/composition.md`).
