# Video and audio conventions

- `script.json` quotes the lesson word for word; a sentence marked `rule` equals a `note` or `caption` of the lesson, and an element of `index.html` that shows rule text carries `data-rule-text`. `pnpm video:check` enforces both and compares the script with the real subtitles.
- An overview narration opens like a video: the first sentence of `overview.hook` greets the child as "bạn" (`pnpm narration:build` stops otherwise; `pnpm video:check` also checks the lead-in). Lessons narrated before the rule carry `"narrationOpeningExempt": true` in `video/projects/<lesson>/media.json` and are never rewritten for it; the rule applies when a lesson's narration is first written.
- If a check forces a change that affects video quality (cutting a sentence, changing pace, dropping an effect), stop and ask the owner.
- Never in git: `video/projects/**/audio/`, `video/projects/**/renders/`, `video/.cache/`, `public/media/`, the TTS environment (`video/.venv`, `video/.python`, `video/.hf`). Sentence takes are the source of the narration: do not delete `audio/*.wav` of a finished video, because re-synthesis gives a different voice take.
- A new shared clip prefers a downloaded effect (free licence, short, child-friendly) imported as a `file` entry in `scripts/lib/sound-spec.ts` with its `credit` (title, URL, licence); generate (Gemini voice line or tone) only when none fits.
- Shared app sounds in `public/sounds/` are committed and never regenerated by `pnpm sounds:build` unless a line changed; `manifest.json` hashes are tested.
- A new shared sound effect (UI click, mascot or avatar sound, celebration; not lesson narration or video voices) starts with a ready-made clip from the internet, for example pixabay.com/sound-effects (Pixabay license) or myinstants.com (approved by the owner). Pick short, child-friendly clips and import them through the shared-sound `file` pipeline: copied into `assets/sounds/`, trimmed, loudness-normalized, hashed in `public/sounds/manifest.json`. Record each clip's source URL and license next to its config entry. Generate the sound (Gemini TTS or a synthesized tone) only when no suitable clip exists. Downloading is allowed; never upload. Lesson narration and video voices keep their own pipeline.
- One item, one engine: an overview narration or a video is read start to end by one voice. Gemini narration that runs out of quota on every key is read again whole by the lesson's video voice, never mixed with the Gemini sentences (`video/lib/narration.ts` `readWithFallback`). Gemini keys live only in `~/.config/gemini/`; never print or commit them.
- Writing to the media bucket or any R2 bucket needs the owner's go-ahead each time.
- Pacing for a slow child: a video not listed in `video/pacing-exempt.json` is short, one idea per sentence, asks before it reveals (`pause: "ask"`), pauses after key reveals (`pause: "think"`) and carries `checkpoint` sentences where the player waits for "Tiếp"; `video:build` and `video:check` enforce it. Never rebuild an existing video to follow it unless the owner says so.
