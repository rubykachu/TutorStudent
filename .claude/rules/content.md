# Content conventions

- Layout: `content/<subject>/<series>/<slug>/lesson.json` (plus `review.md`, and `source-passage.txt` for subjects with `rules.verbatimPassage`). Per-subject settings exist only in `content/subjects.json`; nothing in code names a subject.
- Published ids never change. Retire one in `content/ids.lock.json`; `pnpm content:lock <lesson>...` adds the new ids of the named lessons (without names: every lesson, skipping any whose review hash is stale); agents always name their lesson.
- Editing a published lesson changes its review hash, so `content:check` fails until a review approves it again (`pnpm content:hash <lesson> --approve`, run by `lesson-review`). Generated narration metadata (`overview.narration`: audio and caption paths, the voice that read it) is left out of the hash, so `pnpm narration:build` never needs a new review. Rounds from 3 review only what `pnpm content:diff <lesson>` shows.
- A `note` with `rule: true` is a rule sentence: the section recap repeats it word for word, and any recap sentence close to it must be identical. A `group` with `guide: "<interaction>"` teaches that interaction before the first exercise that uses it, in this lesson or an earlier one.
- `choice` exercises with computable options carry `check` (`relation`: `equal`, `notEqual`, `max`, `min`, `holds`, `fails`); subjects with `rules.checkExpr` need `check.expr` on every `numeric`. Lint recomputes all options, so a distractor that also satisfies the prompt fails.
- A `numeric` exercise whose answer is negative (a value, or the base of a power) needs `"allowNegative": true`: the number pad shows its "−" key only then, and `[check-expr]` fails without it. The flag may also be set on a non-negative answer so the key does not give the sign away. Grading reads "−5" and "-5" alike.
- Every gradable exercise of a new lesson carries `explain` (why the answer is right; at most 3 sentences, optional `tex`, `visualId`, and for `choice` a one-sentence `wrong` reason per tempting option). `content/legacy-lessons.json` lists the lessons written before that rule: `"exempt"` is never asked, `"warn"` gets one warning counting the exercises still without `explain`; delete a lesson from the file once its explanations are done, and never add one to silence the lint. A published lesson without `overview` fails the lint unless it is listed there.
- Tips: a `tip` block is one screen of a section; `content/<subject>/<series>/<slug>/tips.json` holds more tips for a lesson, published or not, without touching `lesson.json` (its hash, videos and narration stay). The tips file has its own `status` and `reviewedHash`: review it with `lesson-review`, then `pnpm content:hash <lesson> --tips --approve`; editing it afterwards fails `content:check` until approved again. Tip ids (`<lesson>.tip.<name>`) are locked with the lesson's other ids. A trick must hold for every input of its problem type; the reviewer tests it.
- Review lessons: `kind: "review"` is the textbook's chapter review. It has no `number`, and its `order` is the previous lesson's number + 0.5 (12.5 sits between Bài 12 and Bài 13). An exercise with `bookRef` (e.g. "SBT 2.58") carries the book's wording and numbers; the lint skips `[textbook-copy]` for the lesson, exempts those prompts and options from `[length]`, and `[book-ref]` fails a `bookRef` outside a review lesson and outside a book-practice section (next bullet). All other text rules still apply.
- Book-practice section: the owner can ask that a regular lesson end with the workbook's own exercises (they appear in tests). The last section carries `"bookPractice": true`, is titled "Bài tập sách bài tập", and reproduces every exercise of the workbook (SBT) for that lesson, word for word, from the pages `sources/math/<slug>/sbt-pNN.png`. At most one per lesson, never in a `kind: "review"` lesson. Each exercise in its `checkIds` then `practiceIds` is one of two kinds. A book exercise has `bookRef` ("SBT 3.12a": book, exercise number, sub-item letter), unique in the lesson, and `explain` (with `wrong` per tempting `choice` option) even in a lesson listed in `content/legacy-lessons.json`. A lead-in step is a guided step in the lesson's own words, with no `bookRef` and `"leadsTo": "<id of the book exercise>"`: it must name the next book exercise after it in that order, at most 2 per book exercise, and a lead-in's text keeps every rule (`[textbook-copy]`, `[length]`, `[explain]`). Lint `[book-practice]` checks all this (an exercise with neither `bookRef` nor `leadsTo` fails; `leadsTo` anywhere else fails). `[textbook-copy]` and the `[length]` limits skip only the book wording of book exercises (`prompt`, options, segments, pairs, items), and `[book-ref]` allows `bookRef` only there. Hints, `explain`, recaps, tips and the section's own blocks are the lesson's words and keep every rule. Step-by-step guidance for a book exercise is its lead-ins (easy to hard, reaching the same skill), its three hint levels and its `explain`. The section may hold more exercises than the usual per-section limit. `content:check --stats` prints each lesson's `bookRef`s, to audit coverage against the workbook's pages. Procedure: skill `lesson-author` ("Phần bài tập sách bài tập"); verification against the pages: skill `lesson-review`.
- Readability pass: every new lesson, and every later change to a lesson's child-facing text, gets a Haiku "đọc hiểu" pass (`model: "haiku"`, items only, output `.shots/review/<slug>/doc-hieu.md`) after the full review rounds and before `content:hash --approve`; the author rewrites every item not labelled "Hiểu rõ". Procedure in skill `lesson-review`; never run it over existing lessons unless the owner asks.
- Wording, number format and sentence length rules apply to subjects with `language: "vi"`.
- Never copy textbook text or pictures, except verbatim reading passages with their source, and the exercise prompts of a lesson with `kind: "review"` or of a book-practice section, marked with `bookRef`; their recaps, hints, explanations and tips are still the lesson's own words. `sources/` is never committed.
- Before authoring or reviewing, read `docs/lessons-learned/index.md`. A reviewer who finds a Nghiêm trọng error already listed there bumps its count and adds the example; a new kind gets a new entry.
